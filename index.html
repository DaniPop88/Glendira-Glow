p. 14 · Letter from the founder

Rooted in Lobo, built on resilience.

Founded by Glenda Perez Ariola, Glendira Glow Collective began as a small community shop in Lobo, Batangas. Inspired by Filipino resilience and creativity, we built our brand on quality apparel, fair pricing, and customer care.

Our mission is simple. To provide stylish, affordable apparel for every Filipino. To support local suppliers and promote sustainable clothing practices. To create a brand rooted in Filipino culture and hospitality.

— Glenda Perez Ariola, founder
